Cockroaches

Kitchens, drains and food areas. Treated where they hide and breed, not only where you see them.

Signs to look for

  • Small dark droppings that look like ground pepper
  • Egg cases behind appliances and in cupboards
  • A musty smell in kitchens and storerooms
  • Cockroaches running when you switch on a light
Book a treatment

Rats and mice

Baiting, trapping and sealing the gaps they use to get in.

Signs to look for

  • Droppings along walls, in cupboards or the roof
  • Gnawed packaging, wiring or wood
  • Scratching sounds in the ceiling at night
  • Greasy rub marks along skirting boards
Book a treatment

Termites

Inspection of timber, walls and soil, then treatment to protect the structure.

Signs to look for

  • Mud tubes on walls, foundations or wooden frames
  • Timber that sounds hollow when tapped
  • Small piles of discarded wings near windows
  • Doors and window frames that suddenly stick
Book a treatment

Bed bugs

Mattresses, bed frames and furniture seams, with a follow-up visit.

Signs to look for

  • Itchy bites in lines or clusters after sleeping
  • Small blood spots on sheets and pillowcases
  • Dark spots along mattress seams and bed frames
  • A sweet, musty smell in the bedroom
Book a treatment

Ants

Trails traced back to the nest so they stop coming back.

Signs to look for

  • Steady trails to food, sugar or pet bowls
  • Small soil piles along paving and walls
  • Winged ants indoors after rain
Book a treatment

Fleas and ticks

Indoor and garden treatment, timed with your pets’ own treatment.

Signs to look for

  • Small itchy bites around ankles and legs
  • Pets scratching and grooming more than usual
  • Tiny dark specks in pet bedding
Book a treatment

Flies and mosquitoes

Breeding sites found and treated, plus screening advice.

Signs to look for

  • Flies gathering around bins and food areas
  • Mosquitoes breeding in standing water, gutters or pots
  • Maggots in waste areas
Book a treatment

Before and after a treatment

The exact steps depend on the pest and the property. Your confirmation note from 4Guard will tell you what applies to you.

Do we need to leave the property?

For some treatments and for fumigation, people and pets need to stay out for a period. If that applies, your confirmation will say for how long.

How do I prepare?

Usually: clear kitchen cupboards and surfaces, cover or remove food, keep pets away from treated areas and give us access to the roof or outbuildings if needed. We send the details with your confirmation.

How soon will I see results?

Many pests start dying off within days. Bed bugs, termites and heavy infestations often need a follow-up visit, which we will explain when we confirm.

Do you treat businesses?

Yes. We treat offices, restaurants and food businesses, shops, warehouses and other commercial properties, and can work around trading hours.

I am not sure what pest it is.

Choose Not sure / other pest when you book. We will inspect, tell you what we find and recommend a treatment.
