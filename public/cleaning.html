Good to know

Tell us about the space when you book and we will confirm the scope, the team and the time needed.

Can you clean after a pest treatment?

Yes. Choose pest control and cleaning when you book and we will plan both visits in the right order.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. Add access instructions in the booking details, for example a key with security or a gate code.

Can you clean outside business hours?

For businesses we can plan around your trading hours. Mention your preferred times in the booking details.

Do you bring equipment and products?

Tell us in your booking if you have specific products you want us to use, otherwise we will confirm what we bring.
